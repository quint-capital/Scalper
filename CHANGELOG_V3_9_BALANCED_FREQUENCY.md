# V3.9 — Balanced quality and trade frequency (analysis-only)

Purpose: respond to the request for more high-quality setups than the restrictive V3.8 admission gate allowed, without turning off confirmation.

Changes:
1. Replaced the all-of-three emitted-direction gate with a completed directional M1 close plus at least 2 of 3 cues: M1 structure/displacement, M5 directional support, and M1 momentum.
2. In mixed regimes, allows admission when at least 2 of M15/M5/M1 support the emitted direction and H4/H1 are not both opposed. Existing regime-aligned and structural-transition paths remain.
3. Retains geometry checks, stop-width limits, target rules, paper-only mode, reversed BUY/SELL behavior, one active setup at a time, and per-structural-move duplicate prevention.
4. Uses a separate V3.9 history/journal namespace so results start fresh and do not mix with V2/V3.8.

This is a frequency/quality trade-off hypothesis, not proof of profitability. More setups may be admitted, but only a chronological paper sample can determine whether expectancy improves. No broker orders are sent.
